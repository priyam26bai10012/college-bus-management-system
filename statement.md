## Problem Statement

College bus services manage students, buses, routes, seat allocations, and travel attendance. Managing these records manually can make searching, updating, and preparing reports time-consuming.

The College Bus Management System provides a simple command-line application for organizing these records in one place.

## Scope

The project covers:
- Student record management
- Bus record management
- Route and stop management
- Student-to-bus allocation
- Bus attendance records
- Basic operational reports

The system is designed as a local, terminal-based application. It does not include online payments, GPS tracking, or a web/mobile interface.

## Target Users

- College transport administrators
- Bus coordinators
- Staff members responsible for transport records

## High-Level Features

1. Add, search, update, and remove student records.
2. Add and manage college buses.
3. Create and manage bus routes and stops.
4. Assign students to buses and routes.
5. Record daily attendance.
6. Generate student, route, occupancy, and attendance reports.
7. Store records in JSON files for persistence.
